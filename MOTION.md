# Motion design — 100 points

Every point below is implemented in `assets/css/site.css`, `assets/css/motion.css`, `assets/js/site.js` and `assets/js/motion.js`. Code comments marked `M01`…`M100` refer to these numbers. All motion is switched off for visitors who set "reduce motion" on their device, and pointer-only effects (cursor ring, tilt, magnetic buttons) are skipped on touch screens.

## Loading and page transitions
1. Preloader logo fades up.
2. Preloader percentage counts 0 → 100.
3. Preloader progress bar fills.
4. Five "building" bars rise and fall like a skyline under construction.
5. Loader exits as a split curtain: two navy panels slide up one after the other.
6. Leaving a page: a navy veil wipes up carrying the logo.
7. Page content fades in behind the opening curtain.
8. Red reading-progress bar across the top of every page.

## Header and navigation
9. Header shrinks and turns to blurred glass after scrolling.
10. Header slides away on scroll down and returns on scroll up.
11. Nav underline grows from the left and exits to the right.
12. Nav labels roll: the word slides up and a red copy replaces it.
13. Projects dropdown fades down and its items cascade in.
14. Dropdown caret rotates.
15. Dropdown items indent on hover and their icons nudge.
16. "Contact us" button gets a periodic shine sweep.
17. Logo scales and tilts slightly on hover.
18. Burger icon morphs into an X.
19. Mobile menu opens as a circle expanding from the burger.
20. Mobile menu items rise in one by one, each with its own icon.

## Cursor and pointer
21. A red ring follows the cursor with easing.
