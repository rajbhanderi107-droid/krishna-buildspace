About Us

Who we are

Krishna BuildSpace Ltd. has established itself as a prominent and dependable force in the construction industry, consistently delivering innovative and cutting-edge solutions across diverse sectors. With over 30 years of experience since its foundation in 1995, the company has built a strong reputation for quality, reliability, and timely execution.

With nearly three decades of experience, we have cultivated a distinguished reputation for our steadfast commitment to quality, precision, and efficiency. Our unwavering dedication to delivering value-driven solutions within stipulated time lines and budgets has been the foundation of our sustained growth. This resolute focus has earned us the trust and confidence of a diverse clientele, including government bodies, corporate organizations, private developers & Industries.

Why Choose Us

We deliver expertise you can trust our service

Our greatest strength lies in our people. Our team comprises over 200 highly skilled and experienced professionals, including architects, designers, civil engineers, and specialized workers, all of whom bring extensive expertise in civil engineering. Each division within our organization is led by experts with deep knowledge and proven proficiency in their respective fields, ensuring the highest standards of excellence across every facet of our work.

At Krishna BuildSpace, safety and sustainability are at the core of our mission. We uphold rigorous safety standards to ensure the well-being of our workforce, stakeholders, and the surrounding environment. Equally, we are dedicated to implementing eco-friendly practices that not only reduce our ecological footprint but also enhance the enduring value and impact of our projects.

At the core of our operations is a deep understanding of each client’s unique needs. We collaborate closely with our clients to craft bespoke solutions that align seamlessly with their vision and goals. Through clear communication and a commitment to partnership, we ensure flawless project execution while fostering enduring relationships built on trust and excellence.

Mission & Vision

Krishna BuildSpace Ltd. is shaping India’s future through purpose-driven infrastructure. Every project is an effort to build value—empowering communities, embracing technology, and fostering sustainability.

Our focus on innovation, quality, and meaningful partnerships helps us drive real progress and lasting transformation across sectors.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a forward-thinking ecosystem across infrastructure and allied sectors—driven by innovation, sustainability, and meaningful partnerships that deliver lasting value and transform how communities grow and connect.
