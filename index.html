About Us

Founded in 1995 Krishna BuildSpace Ltd. has established itself as a prominent and dependable force in construction industry. Over the past 30 years, the company has consistently delivered innovative and cutting-edge construction solutions across diverse sectors.

With nearly three decades of experience, we have cultivated a distinguished reputation for our steadfast commitment to quality, precision, and efficiency. Our unwavering dedication to delivering value-driven solutions within stipulated time lines and budgets has been the foundation of our sustained growth. This resolute focus has earned us the trust and confidence of a diverse clientele, including government bodies, corporate organizations, private developers & Industries.
